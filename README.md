**Quantum Error Correction Sonnet** — *Daniel Gottesman*

We cannot clone, perforce; instead, we split<br>
Coherence to protect it from that wrong<br>
That would destroy our valued quantum bit<br>
And make our computation take too long.

Correct a flip and phase — that will suffice.<br>
If in our code another error's bred,<br>
We simply measure it, then God plays dice,<br>
Collapsing it to X or Y or Zed.

We start with noisy seven, nine, or five<br>
And end with perfect one. To better spot<br>
Those flaws we must avoid, we first must strive<br>
To find which ones commute and which do not.

With group and eigenstate, we've learned to fix<br>
Your quantum errors with our quantum tricks.
